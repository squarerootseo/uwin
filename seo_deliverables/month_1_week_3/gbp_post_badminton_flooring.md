## Post Content

Planning to upgrade or build a new indoor badminton facility in India? 

The flooring system you choose directly dictates athlete safety, gameplay consistency, and your facility's long-term operational costs. Two primary options dominate the market: high-performance PVC vinyl and premium sprung wooden flooring.

How do you determine the correct technical specification for your needs?

• **PVC Vinyl Systems:** Highly cost-effective and engineered with point-elastic shock absorption. A minimum 4.5mm thickness is required for professional use to protect athlete joints. Excellent for commercial pay-and-play arenas and school sports halls.

• **Sprung Wooden Systems:** Built using kiln-dried Canadian Maple or Teakwood over an EPDM rubber shock pad matrix. This provides area-elastic shock absorption, critical for protecting heavy adult athletes during intense daily training. It is the gold standard for elite national academies and BWF international tournaments.

Both systems rely heavily on a perfectly level, moisture-proofed M20 concrete sub-base to prevent premature failure from rising dampness.
