## Post Content

Planning a new outdoor tennis or multi-sport court for your school, club, or academy in India?

Choosing the right acrylic sports court specifications is critical to avoid premature surface failure, inconsistent ball bounce, and player joint fatigue. 

Institutional buyers are frequently presented with quotes for 5-layer and 8-layer systems, but what is the practical difference?

• **5-Layer Non-Cushion System:** Comprises a concrete primer, acrylic resurfacer, two textured colour wear coats, and line marking. It provides a firm, fast-to-medium pace surface. It is exceptionally durable, resistant to scuffing, and best suited for school multi-sport playgrounds and basketball courts.

• **8-Layer Premium Cushion System:** Integrates specialised rubberised shock-absorbing layers beneath the colour coats. These cushion layers absorb vertical foot impact by up to 20%, reducing strain on athletes' knees and ankles during intensive daily training. It delivers a medium-to-slow pace preferred by ITF-standard tennis academies.

An acrylic coating is a surface finish - it relies completely on a sound M20 concrete sub-base with proper slope and moisture barriers.
