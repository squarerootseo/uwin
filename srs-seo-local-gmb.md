# 📝 Google Business Profile (GBP) Posting — Full SOP

---

## 🎯 Objective

The **primary objective of GBP posting is NOT direct selling.**

The purpose of GBP posting is to:

- Inform Google that the website is regularly publishing fresh content.
- Strengthen local SEO signals.
- Improve business relevance for target keywords.
- Demonstrate expertise and authority in the industry.
- Keep the Google Business Profile active and updated.
- Educate potential customers through valuable information.

---

## 📅 Posting Frequency

- Publish **1 GBP Post every week**.
- Every GBP Post should be based on a **newly published or recently updated** website page or blog.
- GBP Post must be published on the **same day or within 24 hours** of publishing the website content.

---

## 🔗 Content Source

Every GBP Post will be created from a **reference URL** provided by the team.

**Accepted reference URL types:**

- Service Page URL
- Blog Post URL
- Industry Guide
- Company Update
- Case Study
- Informational Article

> The URL becomes the **primary source of information**.
> **Never write a GBP post without reviewing the reference URL first.**

---

## 🔍 Step 1: Analyze the Reference URL

Before writing, **carefully read the complete page** and identify the following:

### 1. Main Topic

Determine the primary subject discussed on the page.

Examples:
- A service being offered
- A product category
- A business solution
- An industry challenge
- An educational topic
- A company update

### 2. Target Audience

Identify who the content is intended for.

Examples:
- Consumers
- Business owners
- Students
- Professionals
- Homeowners
- Industry decision-makers

> Understanding the audience helps determine the **tone and angle** of the GBP post.

### 3. Key Takeaways

Extract the most important information from the page. Look for:

- Benefits
- Features
- Solutions
- Insights
- Tips
- Best practices
- Trends
- Frequently asked questions

### 4. Target Keywords

- Identify the **primary keywords and secondary keywords** naturally used on the page.
- These keywords should be **incorporated naturally** into the GBP post without keyword stuffing.

### 5. Geographic Relevance

- Identify any **location references** if applicable (City, Region, State, Service Area).
- If the page targets a specific location, **mention it naturally** within the GBP post.

### 6. User Intent

Understand why someone would visit the page. Common intents include:

- Learning about a topic
- Finding a service provider
- Comparing solutions
- Exploring options
- Getting expert guidance
- Solving a problem

> The GBP post should **align with the same user intent** as the source page.

---

## 💡 Step 2: Decide the Content Angle

**Do not copy the website content.**

Instead, transform the source content into a short, informative GBP update.

**Possible content angles include:**

- Educational insights
- Industry trends
- Helpful tips
- Frequently asked questions
- Common challenges and solutions
- Best practices
- Key takeaways from the article
- Recent updates
- Benefits of a service or solution
- Awareness content

> **The objective** is to provide value while encouraging users to learn more through the website link.

### 🥇 Golden Rule

> A GBP Post should **never feel like an advertisement**.
> It should feel like a **useful update** that educates, informs, or helps the reader — while reinforcing the relevance of the website content and keeping the Google Business Profile active.

---

## ✍️ Step 3: Writing Guidelines

### Tone

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
| :--- | :--- |
| Informative | Pushy sales language |
| Educational | Exaggerated claims |
| Professional | Clickbait |
| Helpful | Aggressive promotion |

### Writing Style

Write like an **industry expert**. The post should:

- Explain
- Educate
- Inform

It should **NOT merely advertise**.

---

### 📐 Structure

#### 1. Opening
Introduce the topic clearly.

> *Example: "In textile manufacturing facilities, lint accumulation can impact both efficiency and workplace safety."*

#### 2. Main Content
Discuss:
- The problem or challenge
- Why it matters (importance, industry impact)
- The recommended solution or insight

#### 3. Service Relevance
Connect the topic naturally with the company's expertise.

> *Example: "Professional industrial housekeeping helps maintain cleaner production areas and supports operational efficiency."*

#### 4. Closing (Soft CTA)
End with a soft, non-aggressive call-to-action.

**Examples:**
- "Learn more on our website."
- "Explore our industrial cleaning solutions."
- "Contact our team for guidance."
- "Discover how professional housekeeping can support your operations."

**Avoid:**
- Limited-time offers
- Excessive promotions
- Multiple exclamation marks
- Hard selling

---

### 📏 Content Length

| | Characters |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ideal** | ~1200 |
| **Minimum** | 600 |
| **Maximum** | 1500 |

The post should remain **concise and easy to read**.

---

### 📋 Formatting Guidelines

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
| :--- | :--- |
| Short paragraphs | Large text blocks |
| Bullet points | Keyword stuffing |
| White space | Excessive emojis |
| Readable structure | ALL CAPS writing |

---

### 🔑 Keyword Usage

- Use the **primary keyword naturally**.
  - *Example: "Industrial Cleaning in Indore"*
- Mention **1–2 times** in body, **1 time** in heading.
- **Avoid keyword stuffing.**

---

### 📌 GBP Post Formula

```
Heading
   ↓
Industry Problem
   ↓
Importance
   ↓
Solution
   ↓
Business Expertise
   ↓
Soft CTA
```

Every GBP Post **must include a custom image**. Do not use random stock images whenever possible.

### Recommended Image Specifications

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Size** | 1200 × 900 pixels |
| **Format** | JPG or PNG |
| **File Size** | Below 5 MB |

### Image Design Rules

The image should support the content topic. Include:
- Relevant industry visual
- Business branding
- Topic headline
- Clean layout
- Company logo

### Brand Consistency

Use:
- Company colors
- Company logo
- Consistent font style

Maintain the **same visual identity** across all GBP posts.

## 🤖 AI Image Generation Prompt Framework

Every GBP post must include a relevant, professional, and high-quality image that visually supports the content being shared. The image should represent the topic of the reference URL and align with the brand's professional identity.

### Standard AI Image Prompt Template

> *"Create a professional business marketing image about **[TOPIC]**. Show **[RELEVANT SUBJECT OR SCENE]** in a realistic environment. Professional photography style, high resolution, clean composition, modern and professional appearance, natural lighting, visually appealing, commercial quality, suitable for Google Business Profile posting. Include ample space for text overlay, realistic details, professional branding style, high-quality business marketing visual, 1200×900 pixels, 4:3 aspect ratio."*

### Elements to Include (When Relevant)

- Professionals at work
- Modern business environment
- Industry-related tools or equipment
- Customer interaction
- Team collaboration
- Technology and innovation
- Service delivery
- Educational or informative concepts
- Real-world business scenarios

### Elements to Avoid

- Cartoon-style graphics (unless specifically required)
- Excessive text inside the image
- Clickbait visuals
- Low-quality stock-image appearance
- Unrealistic claims or misleading imagery
- Overcrowded compositions
- Excessive promotional elements

### Final Image Rule

> The image should **visually communicate the main topic** of the website page while maintaining a professional, trustworthy, and business-focused appearance. The goal is to support the GBP post content, improve engagement, and strengthen brand credibility.
